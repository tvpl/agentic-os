# SDK do core — fachada de execução, barril público, estado por instância e pacote publicável

## Problem

Hoje só a API e a CLI sabem rodar um agente de ponta a ponta. A lógica que decide se uma execução
pode começar, onde ela roda e o que ela faz depois mora em `apps/api/src/routes/common.ts`
(`submitPromptRun`, `launchPromptRun`, `launchSkillRun`, `claimRun`, os recibos de `submissionId`), em
`apps/api/src/approvalActions.ts` (`resolveApproval`, a separação de deveres do dispositivo), em
`apps/api/src/routes/runs.ts` (o `retry` com recibo) e em `apps/api/src/routes/skills.ts` (as
validações de uma skill). Quem usa o core como biblioteca recebe `createEngine()` e `engine.runs`
(`RunManager`), e o único jeito de iniciar uma execução é `engine.runs.create` + `engine.runs.execute`:
esse caminho não passa pelo gate de escrita, não cria a conversa, não aplica o orçamento da skill, não
tem idempotência e não conhece `startedBy`. O teste de motor-composicao que prova o "embutidor" (C14 de
`.specs/features/motor-composicao`) roda exatamente por esse atalho. A regra de segurança está no core,
o caminho que a aplica está na API.

O barril `core/src/index.ts` exporta 603 nomes (375 valores e 228 tipos): o motor, mas também o
spawn bruto (`safeSpawn`, `setProviderBinaries`), os leitores de arquivo, o relógio das sentinelas,
o empacotador de skills, o cliente MCP, as constantes de migração. Tudo é "API pública" por omissão;
nada pode mudar sem quebrar alguém, e não há como dizer o que é estável.

O estado de módulo faz dois motores no mesmo processo se pisarem. Achados no código real:
`events` (o barramento, `core/src/events.ts`) é um só para o processo, e oito módulos emitem nele sem
saber de qual motor são; `providerBinaries` e `probeEnvDrop` (`core/src/spawn/safeSpawn.ts`) são a
allowlist de executáveis e o filtro de ambiente dos probes, reescritos por qualquer `MordomoEngine`
construído (o segundo motor, com outro registro de provedores, troca a allowlist do primeiro);
`cache` em `core/src/memory/related.ts` é uma memória de arestas chaveada só por `(id, mtime)` dos
arquivos, então dois bancos com os mesmos ids e mtimes devolvem as arestas um do outro; e o lock de dono
(`core/src/ownerLock.ts`) trata qualquer lock com o PID do próprio processo como obsoleto, de modo que
um segundo `start()` sobre a mesma home no mesmo processo toma o lock do primeiro em silêncio e os dois
passam a ligar timers e recuperar execuções um do outro.

O pacote também não é publicável: `core/package.json` é `private`, tem `exports` de uma linha sem
`types`, sem `files`, sem `engines`, sem `license`, sem README; `npm pack` hoje levaria `src` ou nada,
dependendo do cwd, e `@mordomo/contracts` (de que o core depende) é igualmente privado.

Quem paga: quem quer embutir o motor (um app desktop, uma extensão de editor, um serviço) e hoje ou
copia a API inteira ou burla o gate; o mantenedor, que não consegue mudar um interno sem medir quem o
importava; e o dono, porque o produto comercial (AD-007) é vendido sobre este core. Evidência:
revisão de arquitetura de 07/10/2026 (`review-arch`, item S4 do roteiro) e a leitura do código acima;
não há relato de usuário.

Quando isto entra: um programa que importa só `@mordomo/core` e um provedor sobe um motor, inicia uma
execução com `engine.execution.startPrompt`, acompanha eventos e aprova a escrita parada, com as mesmas
regras da API; dois motores no mesmo processo não compartilham nada; `npm pack` produz um pacote que
instala e roda em um projeto vazio.

## Flow

Reutiliza o que já decide: `MordomoEngine` (`gateWrite`, `assertWriteAllowed`, `resolveCwd`,
`skillRunFields`), `RunManager` (`create`, `execute`, `assertMayStart`, `cancel`, `onEvent`,
`eventsFor`), `ApprovalStore`, `SessionStore`, `SkillCatalog.buildRunPrompt` e as tabelas
`prompt_submissions` e `run_action_receipts`; nada disso é reescrito, só ganha uma porta única.

1. Embutidor (ou `AppContext`) → `createEngine(opts)` em `core/src/sdk.ts` (door 1) → `MordomoEngine`
   (`core/src/engine.ts`, exists): constrói cada store com o `EventBus` do próprio motor (door 3) e uma
   `SpawnPolicy` própria (door 4); `engine.events` e `engine.execution` são as duas portas públicas.
2. `engine.execution.startPrompt | startSkill | retry` em `core/src/execution/service.ts` (door 1,
   new): valida a entrada (limites em `core/src/execution/limits.ts`, new, sem door), resolve provedor
   e sessão, chama `RunManager.assertMayStart`, `engine.resolveCwd` e `engine.assertWriteAllowed`
   (exist), aplica `engine.gateWrite` (exist), reserva o recibo de `submissionId` e lança.
3. Lançamento (`core/src/execution/launch.ts`, new, a lógica de `launchPromptRun`/`launchSkillRun`/
   `claimRun` movida de `apps/api/src/routes/common.ts`): reaproveita a linha parada em
   `waiting_approval` ou cria a execução, monta o prompt (`SkillCatalog.buildRunPrompt`, exists) e chama
   `RunManager.execute` (exists) sem esperar.
4. `engine.execution.resolveApproval` (door 1, a lógica movida de `apps/api/src/approvalActions.ts`):
   `engine.expireStaleApprovals` (movida de `AppContext`), separação de deveres do dispositivo,
   `ApprovalStore.resolve` (exists), lançamento da execução parada ou cancelamento dela, e
   `approval.resolved` no barramento do motor.
5. Acompanhar: `execution.get | list | cancel | events | subscribe | wait` leem `RunManager`
   (`get`, `list`, `cancel`, `eventsFor`, `onEvent`) e o `run.finished` do barramento do motor.
6. Fora: `RunRecord`, `StartedRun`, `ResolvedApproval`; persistência em `runs`, `sessions`, `approvals`,
   `prompt_submissions`, `run_action_receipts` (todas exist, sem mudança de forma).
7. as rotas de `apps/api/src/routes` (`runs`, `sessions`, `skills`, `objectives`, `system`), `approvalActions.ts` e
   `apps/api/src/cli.ts` (todas exist) viram clientes finos: leem o corpo HTTP ou os argumentos, chamam
   `ctx.execution`, mapeiam o resultado para status e texto. `AppContext` (exists) continua
   `extends MordomoEngine` e guarda só token, credenciais, cache de saúde e poller do Telegram.

## Criteria

### S1: Um programa só com `@mordomo/core` roda um agente de ponta a ponta (P1)

`engine.execution` inicia, acompanha, cancela e aprova execuções, e leva consigo as regras de segurança.

**Acceptance Criteria**

1. WHEN a program that imports only `@mordomo/core` registers a provider, calls `createEngine`, enables the provider with `engine.updateSettings`, calls `engine.start()` and `engine.execution.startPrompt({ prompt, provider, startedBy: null })` THEN the call SHALL return `status` `"queued"`, a `runId` and a `sessionId`, and `await engine.execution.wait(runId)` SHALL resolve a `RunRecord` whose `status` is `"done"`
2. WHEN a run has finished THEN `engine.execution.events(runId)` SHALL return its persisted events in order, the last one of type `result`, and `engine.execution.events(runId, afterId)` SHALL return only the events with an id greater than `afterId`
3. WHEN `engine.execution.subscribe(runId, listener)` is called before the provider emits THEN the listener SHALL receive that run's live events in order, and after the returned function is called it SHALL receive none
4. WHEN `engine.execution.cancel(runId)` is called for a run in progress THEN it SHALL resolve `true` and `wait(runId)` SHALL resolve a record with status `"cancelled"`; WHEN the id names no run THEN it SHALL resolve `false`
5. WHEN `engine.execution.get(id)` names no run THEN it SHALL return `null`; WHEN `engine.execution.list({ provider, limit })` is called THEN it SHALL return only runs of that provider, newest first, at most `limit`
6. IF `wait(runId, { signal })` is aborted before the run ends THEN it SHALL reject with the signal's reason and leave the run untouched
7. IF the prompt is empty or longer than 20 000 characters, or `timeoutMs` is outside 10 000 to 3 600 000, THEN `startPrompt` SHALL throw a `ZodError` and create no session and no run

**Independent test:** o exemplo do README do pacote roda e imprime o resultado.

### S2: As regras de segurança moram no core (P1)

Quem usa o core como biblioteca não contorna o gate, a contenção de diretório, os escopos nem a idempotência.

**Acceptance Criteria**

8. WHILE the security profile is `review_before_write`, WHEN `startPrompt` is called with `mode: "write"` THEN it SHALL return `status` `"waiting_approval"`, a `pendingApproval` of kind `write_run` and the `runId` of a run row in `waiting_approval`, and the provider SHALL not be invoked
9. WHEN `engine.execution.resolveApproval(id, "approved", "owner")` resolves that approval THEN the run with the parked `runId` SHALL leave `waiting_approval` and reach `"done"`, no second run SHALL exist, and the result SHALL carry that `runId`
10. WHEN `engine.execution.resolveApproval(id, "denied", "owner")` resolves it THEN the parked run SHALL become `"cancelled"` and the provider SHALL not be invoked
11. IF the security profile is `read_only` and `startPrompt` is called with `mode: "write"` THEN it SHALL throw a `MordomoError` with `statusCode` 403 and `code` `"profile_refused"`, and no session and no run row SHALL exist
12. IF `cwd` resolves outside the home and the enabled indexed folders, or to a folder that contains the home, THEN `startPrompt` and `startSkill` SHALL throw `PathAccessError` and create no session and no run; WHEN `cwd` is absent or is the home itself THEN the run SHALL be created with `cwd` equal to `<home>/workspace`
13. IF `startedBy` is `device:<id>` and the provider's `readConfinement` is not `"workdir"` THEN `startPrompt` and `startSkill` SHALL throw a `MordomoError` with `statusCode` 403 and `code` `"device_provider_refused"` and create no session
14. WHILE the profile is `controlled_write`, WHEN `startPrompt` is called with `mode: "write"` and `startedBy` `device:<id>` THEN it SHALL return `status` `"waiting_approval"`; WHEN `startedBy` is `"owner"` THEN it SHALL return `"queued"`
15. IF `resolveApproval(id, "approved", "device:<id>")` names an approval that same device requested THEN it SHALL throw `statusCode` 403 `code` `"self_approval"` and the approval SHALL stay pending; IF the approval's kind is not `write_run` or `tool_use` THEN it SHALL throw `statusCode` 403 `code` `"owner_only"` and the approval SHALL stay pending, for every member of `ApprovalKind` (the test reads the union from `contracts/src/policy.ts`, so a new kind without a row fails it)
16. IF `provider` names no registered provider THEN `startPrompt` and `startSkill` SHALL throw `statusCode` 400 `code` `"unknown_provider"`; IF it is registered and `settings.providers.<id>.enabled` is false THEN they SHALL throw `statusCode` 400; in both cases no session and no run row SHALL exist
17. WHEN `startPrompt` is called twice with the same `submissionId`, the same body and the same `startedBy` THEN the second call SHALL return the first call's result and no second run SHALL exist; IF the body differs, or `startedBy` differs, THEN it SHALL throw `statusCode` 409 `code` `"submission_conflict"`; IF the first call left a reservation with no result THEN it SHALL throw `statusCode` 409 `code` `"submission_uncertain"`
18. IF `startPrompt` throws after reserving a `submissionId` THEN the reservation SHALL be released and the same `submissionId` SHALL be usable by the next call
19. WHEN `startSkill` is called for a skill that is missing THEN it SHALL throw `statusCode` 404; for a disabled skill, a provider the skill does not list, or a required input that is blank THEN `statusCode` 400; WHEN the skill's daily budget is exhausted THEN `statusCode` 409 `code` `"budget_exhausted"`; in every case no run row SHALL exist
20. WHEN `startSkill` is called for a skill that writes under `review_before_write` THEN it SHALL return `"waiting_approval"` with a `pendingApproval`; otherwise it SHALL return `"queued"` and the run's prompt SHALL be the one `SkillCatalog.buildRunPrompt` builds for that skill with the artifacts folder of the new run
21. WHEN `retry(runId, { submissionId, startedBy })` repeats a prompt run THEN a new run SHALL exist whose `parentRunId` is the first run in the lineage and whose prompt, provider, model, effort, cwd and mode are the source's; IF the source has no recorded request THEN it SHALL throw `statusCode` 409 `code` `"request_unavailable"`; IF the skill it names is gone THEN `statusCode` 409 `code` `"skill_unavailable"`; WHEN called again with the same `submissionId` by the same `startedBy` THEN it SHALL return the first result, by another `startedBy` THEN `"submission_conflict"`
22. WHEN `resolveApproval` is called for an approval past its TTL THEN it SHALL expire it first and throw `statusCode` 409, and the run it gated SHALL be `"cancelled"`
23. WHEN `engine.execution.smokeTest(provider, startedBy)` is called THEN it SHALL run a read-only run of the fixed smoke prompt in `<home>/workspace` and resolve `{ run, passed }` with `passed` true only when the run is `"done"` and an event carries `MORDOMO_OK`

24. WHEN `startPrompt` is called with the `sessionId` of an existing conversation and without `provider` and `cwd` THEN the run SHALL take that session's provider and working folder (checked again against the folders granted now), and IF the session does not exist THEN it SHALL throw `statusCode` 404
25. WHEN `startPrompt` is called with `parentRunId` THEN the new run's `parentRunId` SHALL equal it
26. IF `<home>/workspace` exists and is not a folder THEN `startPrompt` SHALL throw `statusCode` 409 `code` `"workspace_unavailable"` and create no session and no run
27. WHEN `engine.execution.pendingApprovals()` is called THEN it SHALL return exactly the approvals whose status is `pending`, and an approval that was approved, denied or expired SHALL not be in it

### S4: Dois motores no mesmo processo não compartilham estado (P1)

Barramento, allowlist, memórias e travas são de cada instância, e o que é do sistema operacional está listado.

30. 31. 32. WHEN engine A is built with a registry whose provider binary is `alpha` and engine B afterwards with `beta` THEN A's `SpawnPolicy` SHALL still allow `alpha` and refuse `beta`, and B's SHALL allow `beta` and refuse `alpha`

34. WHEN `relatedEdges` is computed for two databases whose files have the same ids and mtimes but different content THEN each SHALL return the edges of its own content, whatever the order of the calls
35. WHILE engine A has called `start()` on a home, IF engine B in the same process calls `start()` on the same home THEN it SHALL throw `EngineLockedError` with `holderPid` equal to `process.pid` and A's lock file SHALL be unchanged; WHEN A has stopped THEN B's `start()` SHALL succeed; WHEN B's home differs THEN both `start()` calls SHALL succeed
36. WHEN engine A holds an exclusive `RestoreLock` on its home THEN an engine over another home SHALL be built without waiting, and WHEN two engines over the same home are built in one process THEN both SHALL be built (the shared lock is shared)

### S6: A fachada segura onde a primeira verificação a atacou (P1)

Emenda 3 (rodada 1 da verificação, 11/10/2026): critérios acrescentados depois de o relatório achar uma regressão HTTP e vinte e um mutantes vivos; cada um tem o check entre parênteses como prova.

**Acceptance Criteria**

47. WHEN `wait(runId)` is called for a run that has already ended THEN it SHALL resolve that run's record at once; WHEN the id names no run THEN it SHALL reject with `statusCode` 404; WHEN it resolves, rejects or is aborted THEN it SHALL leave no listener on the engine's bus (C52)
48. WHEN the recovery of `start()` marks a run `interrupted` THEN `run.finished` SHALL be announced with that status, and a `wait` pending on the run SHALL resolve it (C53)
49. WHEN `retry` is called on the run a `retry` made THEN the new run's `parentRunId` SHALL be the first run of the lineage, and the mode of the retained request SHALL be kept (a write stays a write), for a prompt and for a skill (C54)

WHEN `retry` is called with `startedBy` `null` THEN the receipt SHALL record `program`, the same `submissionId` by `null` SHALL replay and by another asker SHALL be `submission_conflict`; IF the retry throws after its receipt exists THEN the receipt SHALL be removed and the same `submissionId` SHALL be usable (C55)

52. WHILE the provider's `writeConfinement` is `"none"`, WHEN `startPrompt` is called with `mode: "write"` for `"owner"` THEN it SHALL return `"waiting_approval"` under `controlled_write` and `review_before_write` and `"queued"` under `approved_automation` (C57)

55. IF the approval was already decided THEN `resolveApproval` SHALL throw a `MordomoError` `statusCode` 409 `code` `"approval_resolved"`; IF it expired, whether or not it had been swept yet, `"approval_expired"` (C60)
56. WHEN `POST /api/objectives/:id/dispatch` dispatches a prompt step THEN it SHALL answer 200 whatever `settings.limits.defaultTimeoutMs` is (the setting has a floor only), the run SHALL take that default, the prompt SHALL not be held to 20 000 characters (the context packet is added to it), and a `timeoutMs` above 3 600 000 in the body SHALL be answered 400 (C61)
57. WHEN a server closes the streams it has open THEN the streams of another server in the same process SHALL stay open (C62)
58. WHEN `POST /api/runs/:id/retry` is repeated with the `submissionId` of a retry that was parked THEN it SHALL answer 202 with the same body, and 200 for one that was queued (C63)
59. WHEN the notes of a skill are promoted and WHEN a provider's smoke test runs THEN the run SHALL be recorded as started by `owner` (C64)
60. WHEN `cancel(runId, reason)` is given a reason THEN a parked run SHALL record it as its error and a run in progress SHALL record it in an event (C65)
61. WHEN an engine over a home is started THEN another engine over the same folder spelled through a link, and an engine of another copy of the package loaded in the process, SHALL throw `EngineLockedError` on `start()` (C66)
62. WHEN `mordomo run` is given a skill that is disabled, a provider that is not enabled or that the skill does not list, a required input left blank or a skill whose daily budget is spent THEN it SHALL print the reason and exit 1 and create no run; WHEN budget is left THEN the run it starts SHALL be capped at what is left (C67)
63. The routes SHALL take the bounds of a run request (prompt length, timeout range) from `RUN_LIMITS`, and no route SHALL write those numbers as literals (C68)

64. WHILE `settings.limits.defaultTimeoutMs` is above 3 600 000, every way a run is started without a `timeoutMs` of the caller - `startPrompt`, `startSkill`, `retry` of a prompt and of a skill (whether or not the source recorded a timeout), `POST /api/runs`, `/api/runs/:id/children`, `/api/sessions/:id/continue`, `/api/skills/:slug/run`, `/api/runs/:id/retry` of a prompt and of a skill, the dispatch of a prompt and of a skill step, and `mordomo run` - SHALL create the run with that default as it is (C69)
65. WHEN a caller asks for a `timeoutMs` THEN `POST /api/runs`, `/api/runs/:id/children`, `/api/sessions/:id/continue` and `/api/skills/:slug/run` SHALL answer 400 for 3 600 001 and for 9 999 and run with 3 600 000; and every request field named `timeoutMs`, `prompt` or `prompts` of the contract's routes SHALL be built from `RunTimeout` and `RunPrompt`, which are written in terms of `RUN_LIMITS` (C68, C70)
66. WHEN `start()` recovers a run that was `running` that an objective step was dispatched to THEN the step SHALL be `blocked` with `effects_uncertain` true, the reason `Interrupted by service restart` and the next action `Reconcile workspace effects before retrying`, and one `objective.changed` SHALL be announced (C72)
67. WHEN an engine over a home is started THEN an engine over the folder after it was renamed while the first runs SHALL throw `EngineLockedError` on `start()` (C71)

68. WHILE `settings.limits.defaultTimeoutMs` is above 3 600 000, the `AgentRun` the provider is handed by every caller of AC 64, and by `POST /api/skills/:slug/notes/promote`, SHALL carry that default as its `timeoutMs` (C69)

70. The registry of locks SHALL keep one entry per home, find a lock by its real path when the identity of its file cannot be read, compare the token, remove only its own entry on release, and leave no entry after `stop()` (C73)
