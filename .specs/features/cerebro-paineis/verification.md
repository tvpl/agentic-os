1. **The reminders' day is never proven to be the local day. Mutant t3 survived** (`core/src/dashboards/reminders.ts:114`,
   `const day = localDay(now)`).
   - **The mutant:** I replaced `localDay(now)` with the UTC day, `new Date(now).toISOString().slice(0, 10)`. That
     variable keys both the `dedupeKey` and amendment 30's `meta` record.
   - **What stayed green:** C14, C44, C45, C46 and both C99 proofs.
   - **Why:** every reminder proof refreshes between 08:29 and 10:30 in São Paulo. At those hours the UTC date and the
     local date are the same.
   - **What my probe shows:** I ran it in the scratch at 23:30 local, which is 02:30 UTC on the next day. HEAD gives
     nothing again that evening and leaves the 2026-10-31 record empty. Under t3 the probe fails.
   - **The harm under t3:** an evening refresh would remind the same items a second time and spend the next day's cap.
   - **Where the checks say otherwise:** the checks' preamble says the local-day claims are proven where the local
     day and the UTC day differ. C44's and C99's times never make them differ. Amendments 16 and 30 and AC 46 name
     the local day.
   - Severity: major. It is a privacy-neutral correctness rule, but users see it as duplicate reminders.
