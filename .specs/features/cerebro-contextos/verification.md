Every one of the 52 `Proof:` lines was re-run at `e04edd5` in a fresh detached worktree and passes, and each of the 5
faults was killed. The verdict is FAIL anyway, for seven reasons. The `Test policy` bar is not met: no own-layer proof
exists for `contexts.ts` or the run resolution. C22's text contradicts door 8 and AD-040, and the C22 proof asserts the
opposite of what the check says. Three Surface members added mid-build have no check, and one of them (sync
`409 context_unavailable`) has no behavioural test at all. One chip variant drawn by the design has no proof anywhere,
and one placement drawn by the design is never asserted.

## Ranked findings

1. **`Test policy` row 1 is not met: nothing proves `contexts.ts` or the run resolution at their own layer.** The row
   asks for "one at the HTTP boundary **and** one at its own layer; one asserted case per row of the decision table at
   its own layer". I searched `tests/` and `core/src` for direct callers of `contextMembership`, `newContext`,
   `changedContext`, `contextPathStates`, `insideContext`, `checkPaths`, `contextCwd` and `contextDirs`. The only hit
   is `tests/sdk.surface.test.ts`, which lists the barrel names. Every proof enters through `app.inject`. One row of
   door 6's decision table, "comparado como a plataforma compara nomes" (case folding on darwin/win32,
   `core/src/memory/contexts.ts:207`), has no asserted case anywhere. `ContextEnv.platform` exists for tests, but no
   test passes it.
2. **C22 contradicts Landing door 8 and AD-040(d), and its proof asserts the opposite of the check.**
   - C22 says: "with `context=all` files of the whole index; with a run credential of a run without a context, the
     whole index".
   - Door 8 and AD-040(d) say the credential reads only `memory/` and the `ok` paths of its context, and that
     `context=all` means "tudo o que essa credencial pode ler".
   - The proof sides with door 8. `tests/contexts.runs.test.ts:376-379` asserts
     `expect(list).not.toContain(outsideNote)` for `all` and for the run without a context, then
     `expect(await searchPaths(token)).toContain(outsideNote)` for the owner.
   - The check was not reworded, unlike C24 and C36.
   - Door 8 was added in `322d74d` with "Settled mid-build: nothing by the owner". It narrows the literal shape of the
     approved door 7 ("`context=all` volta ao acervo inteiro") and of AC 22. Under `build.md`, a door that contradicts
     an approved row is a renegotiation, not a builder decision.
   - The code is the safer reading. The fix is to reword C22, AC 22 and door 7's meaning with the owner's sign-off,
     not to change the code.
3. **Coverage: three Surface members added mid-build have no check.**
   - (a) `GET /api/sync/plan` and `POST /api/sync/apply` with a context that has no `ok` path → `409
context_unavailable` (the Surface row added in `6a4ee5b`). Only the declaration is tested
     (`tests/contexts.test.ts:1007-1008`). No test drives the status. A grep for `context_unavailable` across the
     tests finds only `contexts.runs.test.ts` (the run routes) and the declaration sweeps.
