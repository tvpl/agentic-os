## Confirmed, with how

- **`new-window.js` cannot be used by a page to do more than the page could do.** Own probe (`gui-probe.sh`-style script, not in the repo; debug build, a copy of the payload with an overlay link, a stub `xdg-open` logging its argument, real `xdotool` input), 21 scenarios on one window.
